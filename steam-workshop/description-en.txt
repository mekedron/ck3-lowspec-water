[h1]Better Water Without Advanced Shaders[/h1]

With the [b]Advanced Shaders[/b] graphics option turned off, the sea in CK3 is a flat colour map: no waves, no light, no reflection. This mod gives the low spec map shaded water — two layers of animated waves, the sun highlight, the sky reflected in the water and a deep/shallow colour shift — while keeping the option off, and for fewer texture reads than the flat water spent.

Pairs with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3800159542]Sharp Terrain Without Advanced Shaders[/url] and its Real Snow add-on; independent of both.

[h2]The cause[/h2]

With Advanced Shaders off the ocean is drawn by Effect waterLowSpec in gfx/FX/pdxwater.shader: the water colour map plus a shore fade, and the shore fade alone reads the heightmap nine times. Nothing else. Lakes are the opposite: they have no low spec effect at all and always run the full water shader, about 50 texture reads per pixel, even on a low spec map.

[h2]The fix[/h2]

One new function, CalcWaterCheap, built from the parts of the vanilla water shader that are cheap: the first two wave normal layers, the sun highlight, the sky cube map through the Fresnel term, the deep/shallow colour and the shore fade from a single height read. Left out: flow maps, foam, approaching shore waves, refraction, the third wave layer, the shadow map and the cloud mask. Six texture reads per pixel against eleven for the flat vanilla water; lakes drop from about fifty to six.

[h2]Notes[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Keep Advanced Shaders off.[/b] With it on the game uses the high spec water and this mod does nothing at all
[*]Ocean and lakes are touched; rivers already use the full water shader in low spec and are left alone
[*]Two switches in gfx/FX/better_water_options.fxh, one for the ocean and one for lakes
[*]Graphics-only: no gameplay, script or history files, so ironman and achievements are unaffected
[*]Safe to add or remove at any time, including mid-campaign
[*]The first map load after enabling is slower: the modified shaders are compiled and cached from scratch
[*]Built for CK3 1.19.x. Shader overrides replace the vanilla files wholesale, so a patch that reworks the water shader will need an update here
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

Replaces gfx/FX/pdxwater.shader and gfx/FX/jomini/jomini_water_default.fxh in full. Conflicts only with mods that edit those same files, mostly water or map graphics overhauls.
